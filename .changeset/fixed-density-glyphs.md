Icons keep a fixed size at every density. Components that sized glyphs with numeric spacing utilities now use the `size-icon-glyph-*` utilities (12, 14 and 16px) or a fixed pixel size, so icons no longer grow or shrink with density. The Switch thumb now moves with a transform only and stays aligned to its track at every density and in right-to-left layouts.
